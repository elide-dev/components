Every shape has an area and a perimeter, measured in one unit.

## Shapes

A shape is closed and flat. Geo has circles, polygons and the shapes you write yourself
([custom shapes](advanced/custom-shapes.md)).

## Units

Lengths are in meters unless you pass a `Units` value. Areas are in the square of that unit.

1. Pick a unit.
2. Measure.
3. Convert, if you need to.
